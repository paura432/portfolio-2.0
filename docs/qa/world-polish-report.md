## Iteration 0 (2026-10-11) · re-baseline and P0 investigation

**Preflight.** Clean tree on the V9 branch; the editorial worktree and the user's dev server on port 3000 untouched; lint, typecheck, 270 unit tests, build green; full E2E 20/21 (the campus "keeps driving" fixed-time check failed under load and passed 2/2 in isolation). QA reports read: V8 final integration, V8 transformer validation, V9 world validation and inventory. Visual state: `docs/screenshots/v9/` (iteration-1 set) and the V8 set.

**P0 — the wedge.** The screenshot (car blocked beside a building, a planter and the sidewalk) was not reproduced from the image; the physics was instrumented instead. A sweep over every 1.5 u grid point of drivable land that is free for the car and has a collider within 2.6 u in at least two directions (736 spots), from 8 headings, trying each of 6 constant inputs (forward, reverse, each with left/right steer) for 3 s with the real `stepCar` and the vehicle's real constraint, found **295 distinct points where the car never gets 2.5 u away**. They cluster on sidewalks beside building padding boxes with a tree trunk or a bench nearby (Duplex at (−18.5, −17…−20), TrustOS at (−30…−33, 26.5) and (−30.5, 7)), in boulevard tree rows and in garden clusters. Several have a best displacement under 0.7 u: genuinely stuck, not merely awkward.

**Root cause (to be confirmed by the fix).** `carPhysics.ts`: heading change per step is `steer · turnRate · min(1, |speed| / 9)`, and a blocked sub-step sets `speed = −0.2 · speed`. Between two rigid bodies the car bumps within a fraction of a unit, never exceeds ≈ 1 u/s, so it cannot turn out, and reversing meets the second body. Trunks and furniture are rigid below their topple / knock speeds, so any forecourt corner with a tree or a bench next to the facade's 1.0 u padding is a trap. The fix belongs to the car model and the level, not to one location.

**Plan for iteration 1 of this loop.** (1) Low-speed steering floor when the throttle is held, so a crawling car still pivots. (2) Glancing contacts slide along the surface (keep tangential speed) instead of reversing; only head-on bumps reverse. (3) A stuck recovery like the boat's: after ≈ 1.5 s of throttle with no displacement, nudge toward the nearest free direction. (4) Level: keep garden clusters and benches out of building forecourt corners (placement margin). (5) The sweep becomes a unit test asserting 0 wedge points and runs on every change. Then camera obstacle avoidance (CAM-01) and the remaining collider categories (COL-02/03).

## Iteration 1 (2026-10-11) · NAV-01 fixed at the root

**Reproduction.** The sweep of iteration 0 (736 tight spots, 295 wedge points) is now `src/game/world/wedgeSweep.ts`; the worst poses are listed in `wedge.test.ts`.

**Root cause, confirmed by the fix.** `carPhysics.ts`: (1) turn authority was `min(1, |speed| / 9)`, zero for a crawling car; (2) every blocked sub-step set `speed = −0.2 · speed`, so a glancing contact with a kerb-side trunk or a facade padding box killed the speed instead of letting the car slide along it. Between two rigid bodies the car never reached the speed it needed to steer out.

**Fix.** (1) `LOW_SPEED_STEER = 0.35`: while the throttle is held, a crawling car keeps 35 % of its turn rate (an arcade pivot; with no throttle a stationary car still does not rotate); the turn direction follows the throttle sign when the car is still. (2) `bumpSpeed`: the push-out direction is the contact normal; a head-on contact (|forward · normal| > 0.6) still bounces back softly; a glancing one keeps `1 − |dot|` of the speed (at least 30 %), so the car slides along walls, kerbs and trunks. No obstacle was removed or shrunk; buildings, trunks and furniture are as solid as before.

**Evidence.** `wedge.test.ts`: the city on a 1.5 u grid from 8 headings, 736 tight spots, 0 wedges (a wedge = fails both the 3 s / 2.5 u trial and the 6 s / 4 u trial; the tree and prop simulations advance with the car and are restored after each trial, so a rammed trunk falls as in the game). One pose in a four-tree garden cluster at (−44, −54.5) passes only the long trial: the car crawls out at low speed; recorded, not hidden. `e2e/navigation.spec.ts`: the five worst poses (Duplex forecourt corner ×2, TrustOS north and south, plaza south exit) staged by teleport, driven out with plain keys (S, W, S+A, W+D, W+A, S+D) within one or two attempts each; 1/1 green. The attempt at a level-side fix (cluster trees 3.2 u apart) was reverted: it reshuffled the procedural layout, lost a tree and created 14 new pockets.

**Regressions.** Unit 272/272, lint and typecheck clean, build green. Full E2E run 1 of this build: 21/22 — the only failure was again `campus.spec.ts` "keeps driving", with the identical displacement as in the V9 iteration-1 run (0.373 u in 1.2 s of wall-clock) and a pass in isolation: as the first test of a run under SwiftShader it gets few frames per second (the physics clamps each frame to 0.1 s), so the fixed budget measured the frame count, not whether driving resumed. The check is now condition-based with the same 0.5 u threshold (hold S until the car has moved 0.5 u, 10 s cap), the same treatment V8.1 gave the smoke check. Run 2 of the build: 22/22 green (desktop + mobile, 7.4 min).

## Iteration 2 (2026-10-11) · P0 hardening: telemetry, multi-contact resolution, regression tests

Triggered by the third brief ("P0: vehicle stuck in city"), which asked for instrumentation, a root-cause check list, iterative contact resolution and dedicated regression tests on top of the iteration-1 fix.

**Instrumentation.** `debugLog.ts` ring buffers (vehicle samples per frame, one entry per contact with before/after position and speed, correction depth, normal, obstacle id and the blocking reason), probe hooks `debug.telemetry / samples / contactLog / clearLog`, and contact normals drawn by the overlay. On demand only; no per-frame console output.

**Root-cause checklist (A–I of the brief), with evidence.** A. Colliders larger than their objects: buildings keep a 1.0 u padding around their footprint (the car's circle, radius 1.3, is shorter than the car's nose, so head-on the body stops ≈ 0.2 u from the facade; sideways the body stops ≈ 1.4 u from it). Judged acceptable for now and recorded as a V9.1 level-design item (a capsule body would tighten both). The hangar (iteration 1) and the tower (iteration 1) were the only colliders larger than their drawn volumes; fixed. B. Overlapping contradictory colliders: none found by the sweep after the fix; touching boxes are covered by `contacts.test.ts`. C. One resolution pushing into another obstacle: possible in V8's single pass; now the pass repeats until stable (`MAX_PASSES` 4) and the building wins unresolvable pockets. D. Projection into a dead end: the drive-area clamp picks the nearest rectangle; `wedge.test.ts` finds no dead end on the city grid. E. Loss of speed or input by state: the wedge mechanism itself (turn authority ∝ speed, bumps reversing speed) — fixed in iteration 1 of this loop. F. CAR MODE without a ground controller: `authorityOf("car")` is the car model and `canControl` only blocks during a pier glide or when moored; the navigation E2E asserts state and authority stay "car" throughout. G. Transformable parts keeping a collider: the vehicle has one circle whatever the body; no part collider exists. H. Destroyed objects colliding: trees and props drop their collider while down (`isObstacle`); `propWorld.test.ts`, `world.spec.ts`. I. Physical vs visual terrain height: one height field (`groundHeightAt`) builds both the meshes and the car's ground; `ground.test.ts`.

**Tests.** `contacts.test.ts` (8): corner, seam, rotated bench vs building (room / pocket), diagonal into a corner then reverse out, wall scrape, reverse into a wall, 30/60/144 fps, double-penetration separation. `navigation.spec.ts` (2): the five former wedges with telemetry (every contact a real thing, no frame jump > 3 u, state and authority "car", badge "car", no Reset); the Duplex forecourt corner driven out, along a free corridor forward 8 u and back 3 u.

**Evidence.** `docs/screenshots/v9/nav-01..07` with `nav-log.json`: the Duplex pose with the overlay, the contact normals while reversing into the facade padding (log: `duplex`, depth 0.018 u, speed −0.53 → +0.11 head-on bounce), the pivot out along the facade (contacts `tree` below topple speed), the sidewalk corridor, and the follow camera's own framing (CAM-01). ## Iteration 3 (2026-10-11) · the whole catalogue destructible; sequence clocks aligned with the physics

**Done.** Bollards (16), bushes (110), signposts and sign posts (9) and light masts (10) joined the simulation with their colliders and reactions; the 19 component-drawn posts share their positions with the components through `fixtures.ts` and are placed and tilted by `DynamicFixture` (plaza signposts, ramp gate masts and signs, Skyport pad masts, hangar sign, harbor gate sign). Furniture impacts emit particles. Bushes yield at walking pace (foliage never traps the car) and any prop whose footprint contains the car's centre yields instead of ejecting it (safe recovery from a pre-existing penetration). The wedge sweep probes eight directions now (diagonal pockets in garden clusters) and still finds 0 wedges.

**Defect found by the full run and fixed at the root.** The V8 beach loop failed once under load: the hull came ashore and the wheels-down sequence finished with the car still 6.9 u out (x = 72.9, expected < 70). Cause: `VehicleController` advanced the transformation and pier-glide clocks by up to 0.4 s per frame while every vehicle model integrates at most 0.1 s per frame, so on slow frames the sequence outran the body. Both clocks now use the physics clamp (0.1 s). This is the kind of frame-rate dependence the brief's F criterion forbids; the ramp launch had the same latent exposure.

**Second defect found under load and fixed at the root (NAV-02).** With the clocks aligned, the ramp loop still failed once in six full runs: telemetry (enabled in the spec, printed only on failure) showed the car at 20 u/s reaching the lip, a `blocked` frame 1.8 u past it and the speed at 13.5 ("Too slow to take off"). The ramp body's end-face rule treated the second of two consecutive sub-steps landing in the 0.8 u band past the lip as a car "coming from beyond" and pushed it forward as a bump. A car past the lip and still moving away from it is now exempt; the end face still stops a car rolling back. `rampColliders.test.ts` sweeps the sub-step phase at 8–20 u/s across the lip; the ramp E2E passed 4/4 under three busy-loop processes (1/3 before the fix).

**Third (NAV-03).** The V8 beach staging spot (26, 46) sits between a street lamp and a garden bush that became solid in this iteration; a car pressed against a bush at rest could never reach the 1 u/s flatten threshold because each blocked sub-step zeroes its speed. Foliage now yields to any forward motion (a parked car touching it does nothing); reproduced and verified in node (`stepCar` + the real constraint) and in the browser.

**Also.** Every plaza-exit bollard pair has a street lamp 0.7 u beside its outer bollard (GEO-04, placement note). The probe now records the vehicle's state transitions (`vehicleStates()`) and the prop events (`props.events()`), so the specs assert the machine's own sequence instead of polled samples and HUD toasts that a later contact can overwrite. The new E2E case for bollard, bush and signpost uses the helpers' 40 s condition-based budget like the rest of the suite (its first 12 s budget timed out under load).
